Copy a template, replace everything in [brackets] and read it out loud once. If it still sounds like you, send it. Each one is under 300 characters before you fill it in. Need a custom one? Use the [free connection request generator](/tools/linkedin-connection-request-generator).

## 1. Shared topic

> Hi [First name], I saw your post on [topic] and it matched something I've been working on. Would be good to have you in my network and swap notes now and then.

## 2. Shared group or event

> Hi [First name], we were both at [event/group]. I enjoyed [specific thing]. Would like to stay in touch.

## 3. Mutual connection

> Hi [First name], [Mutual name] and I worked together on [thing] and they suggested we connect. I work on [what you do], and I think there's overlap with your work at [Company].

## 4. Sales, no pitch

> Hi [First name], I work with [role] at [type of company] on [problem]. I'm following how [Company] handles it. No pitch, I'd just like to connect.

## 5. Recruiter to candidate

> Hi [First name], your work on [specific project or skill] stood out. I'm hiring a [role] at [Company] and wanted to introduce myself. Happy to share details if it's useful, no pressure.

## 6. Founder to peer

> Hi [First name], I'm building [one-line product] and noticed you're doing something similar at [Company]. Would love to compare notes on [specific challenge] some time.

## 7. Job seeker to employee

> Hi [First name], I'm interested in [team or role] at [Company] and your path from [Previous] to [Role] is the kind I'm aiming for. Could I ask you two questions about the team?

## 8. After they viewed your profile

> Hi [First name], thanks for stopping by my profile. I saw you work on [topic] at [Company], so it made sense to connect.

## 9. Content creator to reader

> Hi [First name], I noticed you comment thoughtfully on posts about [topic]. I write about it too and would like to have you in my network.

## 10. Warm follow-up after a call or meeting

> Hi [First name], good to speak earlier about [topic]. I'll send the [thing we discussed] shortly. Connecting here so we can keep in touch.

## Using templates without sounding like one

Change the first sentence every time, because that is the part people recognise. Mention something true about the person. Keep the ask small. More on this is in the [LinkedIn acceptance rate guide](/blog/linkedin-acceptance-rate) and the [outreach guide](/blog/linkedin-outreach-guide).
