Cold means the person has not heard from you before. The aim of the first message is a reply, not a sale. Replace everything in [brackets]. The structure behind these is explained in [LinkedIn cold message structure](/blog/linkedin-cold-message-structure).

## 1. Sales, problem-first

> Hi [First name], teams like [Company] often struggle with [problem]. We built [product] to [specific outcome]. Is this something you're looking at this year?

## 2. Sales, trigger-based

> Hi [First name], saw that [Company] just [hired / launched / raised]. Congrats. When that happens, [problem] tends to get urgent. Open to a quick chat on how others handled it?

## 3. Partnership

> Hi [First name], our audiences overlap: you reach [audience] with [thing], we serve the same people with [thing]. Would a joint [webinar / guide / referral arrangement] make sense?

## 4. Recruiter to passive candidate

> Hi [First name], your background in [skill] is a close match for a [role] we're hiring for at [Company]. It's [one honest selling point]. Worth a short conversation?

## 5. Freelancer or consultant

> Hi [First name], I noticed [specific issue on their site, product or content]. I help [type of company] with exactly that. Happy to send a short written suggestion, free, if you'd like.

## 6. Asking for advice

> Hi [First name], I'm moving into [field] and your path at [Company] is the closest to what I'm aiming for. Could I ask you two quick questions about [topic]?

## 7. Event or podcast invite

> Hi [First name], we're hosting [event/podcast] on [topic] and your take on [specific thing] would help our audience. Would you consider joining?

## 8. Referral request

> Hi [First name], I'm trying to reach someone who owns [function] at [Company]. If that's not you, could you point me to the right person?

## Before you send

Read the message as the person receiving it. Is the first sentence about them? Is there one clear question? If not, rewrite it. Free note drafting: [connection request generator](/tools/linkedin-connection-request-generator).
