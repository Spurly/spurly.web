Copy a template, replace the bracketed parts with something true about the person, and send. Each page has examples for different situations, and none of them needs a tool.

## Templates

- [Connection request templates](/templates/linkedin-connection-request-messages): ten notes under LinkedIn's 300-character limit.
- [Follow-up message templates](/templates/linkedin-follow-up-messages): eight messages for after someone accepts or goes quiet.
- [Cold outreach templates](/templates/linkedin-cold-outreach-messages): eight first messages for sales, partnerships, recruiting and freelancers.

## Need a custom note?

The [free connection request generator](/tools/linkedin-connection-request-generator) writes a short, personal request from a few details about the person.

## How to use templates well

A template saves time on the structure, not on the thinking. Change the first sentence, mention something real and keep the ask small. The full approach is in the [LinkedIn outreach guide](/blog/linkedin-outreach-guide).
