Spurly is AI-powered LinkedIn outreach that runs in the cloud. You connect your LinkedIn account once, the AI drafts your messages, and Spurly finds people, sends connection requests and follow-ups at a safe pace and keeps every reply in one inbox.

## Who builds it

Spurly is built by [Sarthak Vats](/blog/author/sarthak), a full-stack developer. He designs and writes the web app, the backend that runs campaigns and the Chrome extension, and he reads the support email himself. You can find him on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sarthak-vats-793128226).

## How Spurly started

Spurly began as a Chrome extension for capturing leads from LinkedIn. It grew into a cloud product because outreach that only works while a browser tab is open is outreach that stops when you do. Today the extension is one optional feature, and the main product is campaigns, sequences, a unified inbox and an AI writer that runs from Spurly's servers.

## What we believe

- **Honesty about risk.** LinkedIn restricts automation and no tool can promise otherwise. We say so on our [safety page](/product/safety) instead of hiding it.
- **You stay in control.** AI drafts messages and comments, and nothing is sent or posted by AI without your approval.
- **Clear pricing.** One plan, one price, and a free trial you can cancel before you are charged. See [pricing](/pricing).
