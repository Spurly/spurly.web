**Short answer:** HeyReach is built for agencies and teams that run many LinkedIn senders at once. Spurly is built for one person with one account who wants steady outreach at a low flat price. They solve different problems.

| | Spurly | HeyReach |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $24.99/month (₹2,499 in India), one plan | Growth $79 per sender per month; Agency from $999 per month for 25 senders (prices as listed on 3 Oct 2026) |
| Free trial | 7 days; card or UPI required | 14 days, no card |
| Best for | One user, one LinkedIn account | Agencies and teams with many senders |
| Multiple senders | One connected account per user | Many senders in one workspace, with a unified inbox across them |
| Runs in the cloud | Yes | Yes |
| Proxies | Not applicable; no separate proxy to manage | Dedicated proxy per account (on Growth) |
| Sequences | Linear steps: visit, connect, message, follow, like, comment, endorse, wait | Multi-step campaigns |
| Finding leads | Filtered search, companies, posts, jobs, pasted URLs, profile viewers, followers, CSV | Search, import and lists |

## Price

HeyReach prices per sender. On its page it lists Growth at $79 per sender per month on monthly billing, less on quarterly or yearly billing, an Agency plan of $999 per month for 25 senders and a higher Unlimited tier. For a single sender HeyReach costs more than three times Spurly's flat $24.99.

## Who each is for

Choose HeyReach if you run outreach for several people or clients and want one dashboard and one inbox across all of them. That is what it is designed for.

Choose Spurly if you are one person, such as a founder, consultant or recruiter, and want campaigns, a [unified inbox](/product/inbox) and a [lead finder](/product/lead-finder) in one plan at a price that makes sense for a single account. See the [agencies page](/solutions/agencies) for what Spurly does not do.

## Safety

Both run in the cloud and apply limits. HeyReach says it uses a dedicated residential proxy per account and freezes accounts that approach LinkedIn's daily thresholds. Spurly applies daily and hourly connection-request caps and a pause after repeated failures; see the [safety page](/product/safety). No tool can guarantee LinkedIn will never restrict an account. Read [is LinkedIn automation safe?](/blog/is-linkedin-automation-safe).
