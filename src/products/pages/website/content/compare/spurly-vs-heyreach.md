**Short answer:** HeyReach's Growth plan costs $79 per sender per month as of October 2026, with lower prices on quarterly and yearly billing and Agency from $999 a month. Spurly is one $24.99 AI SDR plan for one LinkedIn account. HeyReach is built for agencies and teams that run many LinkedIn senders at once; Spurly is built for one person with one account who wants steady outreach at a low flat price. They solve different problems.

## How much does HeyReach cost?

As of October 2026, HeyReach's pricing page lists Growth at $79 per sender per month, $71 on quarterly billing and $63 on yearly billing. Agency starts at $999 a month ($799 on yearly billing) and Unlimited is $2,999 a month ($2,399 yearly), both for bundles of senders. A Done-for-you tier is custom. The Growth plan includes a 14-day trial with no card, and MCP server access is listed among its features. For a single sender HeyReach costs more than three times Spurly's flat $24.99 (₹2,499 in India).

## Who each is for

Choose HeyReach if you run outreach for several people or clients and want one dashboard and one inbox across all of them. That is what it is designed for.

Choose Spurly if you are one person, such as a founder, consultant or recruiter, and want campaigns, a [unified inbox](/product/inbox) and a [lead finder](/product/lead-finder) in one plan at a price that makes sense for a single account. See the [agencies page](/solutions/agencies) for what Spurly does not do.

## Spurly's AI SDR features

Spurly is an AI SDR for LinkedIn: it finds high-intent leads that match your ICP, writes personalized messages with AI and follows up until they reply, while you approve every message. "Check its site" below means we did not confirm that feature on HeyReach's pricing page, so look before you decide.

| | Spurly | HeyReach |
|---|---|---|
| AI-written messages | AI drafts connection notes and follow-ups in your voice, and you approve every one | Check its site |
| Intent signals | Profile viewers, followers, post authors, hiring companies and people who invite you, each turned into an audience | Check its site |
| Claude (MCP) | Read, draft and act from Claude; acting is off by default and passes the same limits | MCP server access listed on Growth |
| Invitation autopilot | Auto-accept incoming invitations (all, or by headline keywords) and auto-withdraw stale ones | Check its site |
| 24/7 with quiet hours | Runs around the clock with uneven gaps; quiet hours slow sending right down overnight | Cloud-based |

No tool can promise zero risk of a LinkedIn restriction, and that applies to both.

## Safety

Both run in the cloud and apply limits. HeyReach says it uses a dedicated residential proxy per account and freezes accounts that approach LinkedIn's daily thresholds. Spurly applies daily and hourly connection-request caps and a pause after repeated failures; see the [safety page](/product/safety). No tool can guarantee LinkedIn will never restrict an account. Read [is LinkedIn automation safe?](/blog/is-linkedin-automation-safe).
