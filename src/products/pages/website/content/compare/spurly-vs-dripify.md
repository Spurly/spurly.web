**Short answer:** Dripify costs $79 (Pro) or $99 (Advanced) per user per month on monthly billing as of October 2026, or $59 and $79 billed annually. Spurly is one $24.99 AI SDR plan for LinkedIn with everything included. Dripify has more plan tiers, including A/B testing and team features on its higher plans. Both run in the cloud, and both trials start without a card.

## How much does Dripify cost?

As of October 2026, Dripify's pricing page lists two self-serve plans, priced per user: Pro at $79 a month and Advanced at $99 a month on monthly billing, or $59 and $79 a month billed annually. Enterprise is priced through sales. Dripify offers a 7-day free trial with no card required. Spurly is one plan at $24.99 a month (₹2,499 in India) with everything included.

## Spurly vs Dripify

| | Spurly | Dripify |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $24.99/month (₹2,499 in India), one plan | Pro $79, Advanced $99 per user per month ($59, $79 billed annually); Enterprise on request (October 2026) |
| Free trial | 7 days; no card needed | 7 days; Dripify says you can start without entering a card |
| Runs in the cloud | Yes | Yes, "on our servers" |
| Campaigns | Multi-step sequences that stop when someone replies | Unlimited drip campaigns on Pro and Advanced |
| Inbox | Unified inbox with reactions and file sharing, included | Dedicated inbox on Pro and above |
| Extras on higher plans | Everything is in the one plan | HubSpot integration and open API (Pro); team management, lead tagging and A/B testing (Advanced; its page also lists A/B testing as a waitlist feature) |
| Finding leads | Filtered search, companies, posts, jobs, pasted URLs, profile viewers, followers, CSV | Not itemised on its pricing page |

## Trial

Both offer a 7-day free trial, and both say you can start without entering a card. With Spurly you subscribe only if you want to continue after day 7.

## Features by plan

Spurly does not have tiers: campaigns, sequences, the inbox and the lead finder are all in the plan. Dripify's Pro plan includes a dedicated inbox, unlimited drip campaigns, HubSpot and an open API; Advanced adds team management, lead tagging and A/B testing. Dripify lists integrations (HubSpot, an open API) that Spurly does not.

## Account safety

Both tools run campaigns from the cloud and describe measures to protect your account. No tool can promise that LinkedIn will not restrict an account. Spurly's approach is on the [safety page](/product/safety): limits per action, variable gaps, and a stop when someone replies.

## Spurly's AI SDR features

Spurly is an AI SDR for LinkedIn: it finds high-intent leads that match your ICP, writes personalized messages with AI and follows up until they reply, while you approve every message. "Check its site" below means we did not confirm that feature on Dripify's pricing page, so look before you decide.

| | Spurly | Dripify |
|---|---|---|
| AI-written messages | AI drafts connection notes and follow-ups in your voice, and you approve every one | Check its site |
| Intent signals | Profile viewers, followers, post authors, hiring companies and people who invite you, each turned into an audience | Check its site |
| Claude (MCP) | Read, draft and act from Claude; acting is off by default and passes the same limits | Check its site |
| Invitation autopilot | Auto-accept incoming invitations (all, or by headline keywords) and auto-withdraw stale ones | Check its site |
| 24/7 with quiet hours | Runs around the clock with uneven gaps; quiet hours slow sending right down overnight | Check its site |

No tool can promise zero risk of a LinkedIn restriction, and that applies to both.

## Who should pick which

- **Pick Spurly** if you want a flat, low price, every feature included, and sequences that stop on reply.
- **Pick Dripify** if you need A/B testing, multi-team management or a HubSpot integration.
