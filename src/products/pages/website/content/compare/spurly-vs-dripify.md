**Short answer:** Spurly is cheaper and has a single plan with everything included. Dripify has more plan tiers, including A/B testing and team features on its higher plans, and its trial can be started without a card. Both run in the cloud.

| | Spurly | Dripify |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $24.99/month (₹2,499 in India), one plan | Basic $59, Pro $79, Advanced $99 per user per month ($39, $59, $79 billed annually) |
| Free trial | 7 days; card or UPI required | 7 days; Dripify says you can start without entering a card |
| Runs in the cloud | Yes | Yes, "on our servers" |
| Campaigns | Multi-step sequences that stop when someone replies | 1 drip campaign on Basic; unlimited on Pro and Advanced |
| Inbox | Unified inbox with reactions and file sharing, included | Dedicated inbox on Pro and above |
| Extras on higher plans | Everything is in the one plan | Webhooks, HubSpot integration, API (Pro); multi-team, A/B testing, lead tagging (Advanced) |
| Finding leads | Filtered search, companies, posts, jobs, pasted URLs, profile viewers, followers, CSV | Not itemised on its pricing page |

## Price

Spurly is one plan at $24.99 a month (outside India; ₹2,499 in India). Dripify prices per user in three tiers: Basic at $59, Pro at $79 and Advanced at $99 a month, with annual billing bringing those to $39, $59 and $79. The cheapest Dripify tier limits you to one drip campaign and lower daily quotas.

## Trial

Both offer a 7-day free trial. Dripify says you can start without entering a card. Spurly requires a card, or UPI in India, to start the trial, and does not charge until day 8; you can cancel before then and pay nothing. If starting without a card matters to you, Dripify has the advantage here.

## Features by plan

Spurly does not have tiers: campaigns, sequences, the inbox and the lead finder are all in the plan. Dripify unlocks its dedicated inbox and unlimited campaigns on Pro, and A/B testing, multi-team management and lead tagging on Advanced. Dripify also lists integrations (webhooks, HubSpot, an API) that Spurly does not.

## Account safety

Both tools run campaigns from the cloud and describe measures to protect your account. No tool can promise that LinkedIn will not restrict an account. Spurly's approach is on the [safety page](/product/safety): limits per action, variable gaps, and a stop when someone replies.

## Who should pick which

- **Pick Spurly** if you want a flat, low price, every feature included, and sequences that stop on reply.
- **Pick Dripify** if you want to start the trial without a card, or you need A/B testing, multi-team management or a HubSpot integration.
