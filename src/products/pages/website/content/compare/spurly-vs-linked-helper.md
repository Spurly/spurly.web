**Short answer:** Linked Helper costs from $8.25 per license per month on prepaid terms as of October 2026, and the software runs on your own computer or server. Spurly is one $24.99 AI SDR plan that runs on our servers, so there is nothing to install or keep switched on. The trade-off is setup effort and control against convenience.

## How much does Linked Helper cost?

As of October 2026, Linked Helper's pricing page lists Standard and Pro plans from $8.25 per license per month, prepaid for a 1, 3, 6 or 12-month term (discounts of 11%, 33% and 45% on longer terms). A cloud-storage option starts at $16.50 per month, but that only changes where your data is stored; the automation still runs from your machine. The 14-day trial gives full Pro access with no card, and nothing renews automatically unless you turn auto-renewal on. Spurly is one plan at $24.99 a month (₹2,499 in India).

## Where it runs

Linked Helper is software you install. It runs on your own computer or on a virtual server you rent, which gives you control but also means the machine has to stay on and connected. Spurly runs on our servers, so campaigns keep going when your laptop is closed. More on the trade-off in [cloud vs extension LinkedIn automation](/blog/cloud-vs-extension-linkedin-automation).

## Limits

Linked Helper's Standard plan caps actions at 20 per day, while its Pro plan removes the daily caps and leaves the pacing to you. Spurly applies daily and hourly connection-request limits by default; see the [safety page](/product/safety). Removing caps is not the same as being safe: LinkedIn's own limits still apply.

## Spurly's AI SDR features

Spurly is an AI SDR for LinkedIn: it finds high-intent leads that match your ICP, writes personalized messages with AI and follows up until they reply, while you approve every message. "Check its site" below means we did not confirm that feature on Linked Helper's pricing page, so look before you decide.

| | Spurly | Linked Helper |
|---|---|---|
| AI-written messages | AI drafts connection notes and follow-ups in your voice, and you approve every one | AI credits listed on its plans |
| Intent signals | Profile viewers, followers, post authors, hiring companies and people who invite you, each turned into an audience | Check its site |
| Claude (MCP) | Read, draft and act from Claude; acting is off by default and passes the same limits | Check its site |
| Invitation autopilot | Auto-accept incoming invitations (all, or by headline keywords) and auto-withdraw stale ones | Check its site |
| 24/7 with quiet hours | Runs around the clock with uneven gaps; quiet hours slow sending right down overnight | Runs on your own computer or server, so it has to stay on |

No tool can promise zero risk of a LinkedIn restriction, and that applies to both.

## Who should choose which

Choose Linked Helper if you want the lowest price, like tuning settings and are comfortable running software yourself. Choose Spurly if you want to connect your account and be done.
