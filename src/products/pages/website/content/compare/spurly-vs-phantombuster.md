**Short answer:** PhantomBuster is a toolbox for automating web tasks, including scraping data from LinkedIn. Spurly is a finished AI SDR product for LinkedIn outreach. If you want to build custom data workflows, PhantomBuster fits. If you want to send connection requests and follow-ups and read replies, Spurly is simpler.

| | Spurly | PhantomBuster |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | LinkedIn outreach campaigns | General automation and data extraction, including LinkedIn |
| Price | $24.99/month (₹2,499 in India) | Plans priced by execution hours; Start is $69/month ($56/month billed annually), Grow $159, Scale $439 |
| Free trial | 7 days; no card needed | A trial is offered; check the page for the length |
| Setup | Connect your account and start a campaign | Pick and configure individual automations, then chain them |
| Inbox | Unified inbox | Not its focus |
| Sequences | Built in; stop when someone replies | Built by combining automations |
| Safety limits | Daily and hourly connection-request caps, pacing and failure pauses | You set the pacing |

## What each is for

PhantomBuster gives you building blocks. You choose an automation for each job, set how often it runs and decide the limits. That flexibility suits people who want to scrape lists, enrich data or build something custom.

Spurly gives you one workflow. You pick the people, write the messages and start a [campaign](/product/campaigns). Limits are applied for you. You give up customisation and gain simplicity.

## Spurly's AI SDR features

Spurly is an AI SDR for LinkedIn: it finds high-intent leads that match your ICP, writes personalized messages with AI and follows up until they reply, while you approve every message. "Check its site" below means we did not confirm that feature on PhantomBuster's pricing page, so look before you decide.

| | Spurly | PhantomBuster |
|---|---|---|
| AI-written messages | AI drafts connection notes and follow-ups in your voice, and you approve every one | Check its site |
| Intent signals | Profile viewers, followers, post authors, hiring companies and people who invite you, each turned into an audience | Check its site |
| Claude (MCP) | Read, draft and act from Claude; acting is off by default and passes the same limits | Check its site |
| Invitation autopilot | Auto-accept incoming invitations (all, or by headline keywords) and auto-withdraw stale ones | Check its site |
| 24/7 with quiet hours | Runs around the clock with uneven gaps; quiet hours slow sending right down overnight | You set the pacing yourself |

No tool can promise zero risk of a LinkedIn restriction, and that applies to both.

## Safety

Because PhantomBuster leaves pacing to you, how safe it is depends on how carefully you configure it. Spurly applies caps by default; see the [safety page](/product/safety). Neither can promise LinkedIn will never restrict an account. Read [is LinkedIn automation safe?](/blog/is-linkedin-automation-safe).
