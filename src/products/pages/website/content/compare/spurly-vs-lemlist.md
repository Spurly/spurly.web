**Short answer:** Lemlist is primarily an email outreach tool that also includes LinkedIn steps and a lead database. Spurly is LinkedIn only, at a lower price. Choose by whether you need email in the same workflow.

| | Spurly | Lemlist |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $24.99/month (₹2,499 in India), one plan | Email $55/month; Multichannel $87/month (as listed 3 Oct 2026) |
| Free trial | 7 days; card or UPI required | 14 days |
| Channels | LinkedIn | Email, LinkedIn, and more |
| LinkedIn actions | Visit, connect, message, follow, like, comment, endorse | Visits, follows, invites, messages and InMail |
| Lead database | Search LinkedIn, import CSV | Lemlist lists a database of over 650 million contacts |
| Inbox | Unified LinkedIn inbox | Multichannel inbox |
| Add-ons | None | Extras such as lead enrichment (about $16/month) |

## Where Lemlist is stronger

Lemlist combines email and LinkedIn in one sequence and comes with a large contact database. If your outreach mixes channels, or you need to find email addresses, it does more than Spurly.

## Where Spurly is stronger

Spurly costs less, includes everything in one plan, and is built around LinkedIn: [campaigns](/product/campaigns), a [lead finder](/product/lead-finder), a [unified inbox](/product/inbox) and a [safety layer](/product/safety). If LinkedIn is the channel, you are not paying for email features you will not use.

## Safety

Both run in the cloud. Lemlist's own copy does not remove LinkedIn's restrictions on automation, and neither does Spurly's. See [is LinkedIn automation safe?](/blog/is-linkedin-automation-safe).
