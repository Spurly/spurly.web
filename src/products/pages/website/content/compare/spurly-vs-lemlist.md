**Short answer:** Lemlist costs $69 a month for Email and $109 a month for Multichannel as of October 2026 ($55 and $87 billed annually), and LinkedIn automation sits in Multichannel. Spurly is LinkedIn only: one $24.99 AI SDR plan with everything included. Lemlist is primarily an email outreach tool that also includes LinkedIn steps and a lead database, so choose by whether you need email in the same workflow.

## How much does Lemlist cost?

As of October 2026, Lemlist's pricing page lists Email at $69 a month ($55 billed annually) and Multichannel at $109 a month ($87 billed annually). Multichannel is priced per user and includes LinkedIn automation; Enterprise is custom. A 14-day free trial is listed, with a 30-day trial shown as a limited-time offer. Add-ons are sold separately, including lead data enrichment from $16 a month on annual billing ($20 monthly) and a buying-intent-signals add-on from $21 a month on annual billing ($29 monthly). Spurly is one plan at $24.99 a month (₹2,499 in India).

## Where Lemlist is stronger

Lemlist combines email and LinkedIn in one sequence and comes with a large contact database. If your outreach mixes channels, or you need to find email addresses, it does more than Spurly.

## Where Spurly is stronger

Spurly costs less, includes everything in one plan, and is built around LinkedIn: [campaigns](/product/campaigns), a [lead finder](/product/lead-finder), a [unified inbox](/product/inbox) and a [safety layer](/product/safety). If LinkedIn is the channel, you are not paying for email features you will not use.

## Spurly's AI SDR features

Spurly is an AI SDR for LinkedIn: it finds high-intent leads that match your ICP, writes personalized messages with AI and follows up until they reply, while you approve every message. "Check its site" below means we did not confirm that feature on Lemlist's pricing page, so look before you decide.

| | Spurly | Lemlist |
|---|---|---|
| AI-written messages | AI drafts connection notes and follow-ups in your voice, and you approve every one | Check its site |
| Intent signals | Profile viewers, followers, post authors, hiring companies and people who invite you, each turned into an audience | Buying-intent-signals add-on (from $21/month on annual billing) |
| Claude (MCP) | Read, draft and act from Claude; acting is off by default and passes the same limits | Check its site |
| Invitation autopilot | Auto-accept incoming invitations (all, or by headline keywords) and auto-withdraw stale ones | Check its site |
| 24/7 with quiet hours | Runs around the clock with uneven gaps; quiet hours slow sending right down overnight | Cloud-based |

No tool can promise zero risk of a LinkedIn restriction, and that applies to both.

## Safety

Both run in the cloud. Lemlist's own copy does not remove LinkedIn's restrictions on automation, and neither does Spurly's. See [is LinkedIn automation safe?](/blog/is-linkedin-automation-safe).
