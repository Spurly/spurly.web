**Short answer:** Waalaxy has the lowest entry price and offers email sequences on its top plan. Spurly is one flat plan with everything included and no monthly invitation cap tied to the plan. Both now run in the cloud, so that is no longer a difference.

| | Spurly | Waalaxy |
|---|---|---|
| Price | $24.99/month (₹2,499 in India), one plan | Pro €19, Advanced €49, Business €69 per user per month (half that billed annually) |
| Free trial | 7 days; card or UPI required | 14 days |
| Runs in the cloud | Yes | Yes; the browser extension is no longer required for campaigns (since 1 July 2026) |
| LinkedIn invitations | Paced by daily and hourly limits and LinkedIn's weekly allowance | 300 per month (Pro), 800 per month (Advanced, Business) |
| Sequences | Linear steps: visit, connect, message, follow, like, comment, endorse, wait | Pre-built sequences and automated follow-ups |
| Email outreach | Not offered | Cold email sequences and multichannel campaigns on Business |
| Inbox | Unified inbox with reactions and file sharing | "Smart LinkedIn Inbox" with saved replies and scheduling |
| Finding leads | Filtered search, companies, posts, jobs, pasted URLs, profile viewers, followers, CSV | LinkedIn search, Sales Navigator or Recruiter Lite |

## Price

Spurly is one plan at $24.99 a month (outside India; ₹2,499 in India). Waalaxy prices per user in euros: Pro at €19, Advanced at €49 and Business at €69 a month, with annual billing halving those figures. Waalaxy has the cheaper entry point; Spurly includes the full product at its price.

## Cloud or extension

Waalaxy used to be known for working through a browser extension. Its help centre now says that since 1 July 2026 it no longer relies on one and everything runs in its app, with an optional extension for importing. Spurly also runs in the cloud, so neither depends on your laptop being open.

## Invitation limits

Waalaxy plans carry a monthly invitation allowance: 300 on Pro and 800 on Advanced and Business. Spurly has no plan-based monthly cap. It paces invitations with hourly and daily limits and tracks LinkedIn's own weekly allowance; see the [safety page](/product/safety).

## Email outreach

Waalaxy's Business plan adds cold email sequences and multichannel LinkedIn and email campaigns. Spurly is LinkedIn only. If you want email in the same tool, that is a reason to choose Waalaxy.

## Who should pick which

- **Pick Spurly** if you want one flat plan with everything included, sequences that stop on reply, and lead sources such as profile viewers, followers, posts and jobs.
- **Pick Waalaxy** if you want the lowest entry price, a longer trial, or email sequences alongside LinkedIn.
