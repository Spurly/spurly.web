**Short answer:** Waalaxy costs €19 (Pro), €49 (Advanced) or €69 (Business) per user per month as of October 2026, with a 14-day free trial. Spurly is one $24.99 AI SDR plan for LinkedIn with everything included and no monthly invitation cap tied to the plan. Waalaxy has the lowest entry price and offers email sequences on its top plan. Both now run in the cloud, so that is no longer a difference.

## How much does Waalaxy cost?

As of October 2026, Waalaxy's pricing page lists three plans, priced per user per month: Pro at €19, Advanced at €49 and Business at €69. Quarterly billing takes 20% off and yearly billing 50% off. Every plan has a 14-day free trial. LinkedIn invitations are capped at 300 a month on Pro and 800 on Advanced and Business, and the LinkedIn Inbox is a paid add-on. Spurly is one plan at $24.99 a month (₹2,499 in India) with everything included.

## Cloud or extension

Waalaxy used to be known for working through a browser extension. Its help centre now says that since 1 July 2026 it no longer relies on one and everything runs in its app, with an optional extension for importing. Spurly also runs in the cloud, so neither depends on your laptop being open.

## Invitation limits

Waalaxy plans carry a monthly invitation allowance: 300 on Pro and 800 on Advanced and Business. Spurly has no plan-based monthly cap. It paces invitations with hourly and daily limits and tracks LinkedIn's own weekly allowance; see the [safety page](/product/safety).

## Email outreach

Waalaxy's Business plan adds cold email sequences and multichannel LinkedIn and email campaigns. Spurly is LinkedIn only. If you want email in the same tool, that is a reason to choose Waalaxy.

## Spurly's AI SDR features

Spurly is an AI SDR for LinkedIn: it finds high-intent leads that match your ICP, writes personalized messages with AI and follows up until they reply, while you approve every message. "Check its site" below means we did not confirm that feature on Waalaxy's pricing page, so look before you decide.

| | Spurly | Waalaxy |
|---|---|---|
| AI-written messages | AI drafts connection notes and follow-ups in your voice, and you approve every one | Check its site |
| Intent signals | Profile viewers, followers, post authors, hiring companies and people who invite you, each turned into an audience | Check its site |
| Claude (MCP) | Read, draft and act from Claude; acting is off by default and passes the same limits | Check its site |
| Invitation autopilot | Auto-accept incoming invitations (all, or by headline keywords) and auto-withdraw stale ones | Check its site |
| 24/7 with quiet hours | Runs around the clock with uneven gaps; quiet hours slow sending right down overnight | Check its site |

No tool can promise zero risk of a LinkedIn restriction, and that applies to both.

## Who should pick which

- **Pick Spurly** if you want one flat plan with everything included, sequences that stop on reply, and lead sources such as profile viewers, followers, posts and jobs.
- **Pick Waalaxy** if you want the lowest entry price, a longer trial, or email sequences alongside LinkedIn.
