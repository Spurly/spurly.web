Spurly has one plan: **$24.99 per month** (**₹2,499 per month** in India), with a 7-day free trial and every feature included. There are no tiers to compare and nothing to unlock later.

## What is included

Everything below comes with the one plan.

**Find the right people**
- Audience builder: LinkedIn people search with filters
- Leads from companies, posts and pasted LinkedIn URLs
- Your network synced, plus profile viewers and followers as leads
- Email and phone enrichment
- Chrome extension capture and CSV import

**Connect and follow up**
- Campaigns and multi-step sequences, sent at a safe daily pace
- Pause automatically when someone replies
- Message templates with variables and a live preview
- AI drafts for connection notes and messages, reviewed by you before launch

**Reply and stay visible**
- One inbox for all your LinkedIn conversations
- Schedule posts and see likes and comments

## How the 7-day free trial works

1. **Add a card (or UPI in India).** You need one to start the trial.
2. **Use everything for 7 days.** Connect LinkedIn, build an audience and launch a campaign.
3. **Day 8: the plan starts.** You are billed for the first month unless you cancelled before then.

Add a card (or UPI in India) to start your 7-day free trial. You won't be charged until day 8. Cancel anytime before then and you pay nothing.

## Why is pricing different in India?

Spurly is priced in rupees for visitors in India (₹2,499 per month) and in US dollars everywhere else ($24.99 per month). Checkout shows the exact price before you pay.

## Teams and organizations

The plan above is per account. If you need Spurly for a team or an organization, the plan is arranged with us directly. [Book a meeting](mailto:founders@getspurly.com?subject=Spurly%20team%20plan) and tell us how many people you have and what you need.

## How to cancel

Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel. Your access runs to the end of the period you have already paid for.
