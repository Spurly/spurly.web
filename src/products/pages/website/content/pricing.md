Spurly is an AI SDR for LinkedIn with one plan and every feature included. The launch offer for the first 100 members is **$14.99 per month** (**₹1,499 per month** in India), locked in for life. The regular price is $24.99 per month (₹2,499 in India). There are no tiers to compare and nothing to unlock later.

## What is included

Everything below comes with the one plan.

- AI-written, personalized messages and sequences
- Intent signals: profile viewers, followers, post authors, hiring companies
- Auto-accept and auto-withdraw invitations
- Works with Claude (MCP)
- Campaigns and multi-step sequences, sent at a safe daily pace
- Pause automatically when someone replies
- One inbox for all your LinkedIn conversations
- Audience builder: LinkedIn people search with filters
- Find leads from companies, posts and pasted LinkedIn URLs
- Your network synced, plus profile viewers and followers as leads
- Email and phone enrichment
- Schedule posts and see likes and comments
- Chrome extension capture and CSV import
- Message templates with variables and a live preview

## How getting started works

1. **Subscribe.** Create your account and pay for the first month with a card, or UPI in India. Your account is active straight away.
2. **Connect LinkedIn.** Build an audience and launch a campaign.
3. **Cancel whenever you like.** Your access runs to the end of the month you have paid for. If you don't renew, your LinkedIn account is disconnected and campaigns pause, but nothing is deleted.

You are charged when you subscribe, then monthly until you cancel. There is no free trial.

## Why is pricing different in India?

Spurly is priced in rupees for visitors in India (₹1,499 per month at launch, ₹2,499 regular) and in US dollars everywhere else ($14.99 per month at launch, $24.99 regular). Checkout shows the exact price before you pay.
