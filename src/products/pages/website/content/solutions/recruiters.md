Spurly helps recruiters turn an open role into a shortlist of candidates and a steady, personal outreach sequence. You search LinkedIn, keep a list per role, send connection requests and follow-ups at a safe pace, and read every reply in one inbox.

## The recruiter's problem

Good candidates are spammed with copy-paste "exciting opportunity" messages every day. Writing something personal for every one of them takes hours, and the replies get lost between LinkedIn tabs and a spreadsheet.

## A recruiter's workflow in Spurly

1. **Turn the role into filters.** Search by title, location and company, then import the people who match. See the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder).
2. **Keep a list per role.** Each open role gets its own audience and campaign.
3. **Reach out like a person.** Send a connection request, wait until they accept, then message with the specifics of the role. See [campaigns](/product/campaigns).
4. **Answer in one place.** Replies stop the sequence and land in your [inbox](/product/inbox).

## Capture from the pages you already use

If you work in Sales Navigator, the [Chrome extension](/product/chrome-extension) captures profiles from those pages into Spurly. If you do not, the lead finder uses standard LinkedIn search.

## Personal at scale

Write a template once and Spurly fills in details such as first name for each person, with a live preview before anything goes out. Personal, relevant notes get replies; blasts get ignored.

## What Spurly is not

Spurly sends connection requests and messages to your connections. It is not an InMail tool.
