Spurly is an AI SDR for founders: you build the product, and it finds high-intent leads on LinkedIn, writes personalized messages with AI and follows up 24/7 until they reply. You approve every message and take the replies yourself, so you get an SDR's pipeline without hiring an SDR.

## The problem

Early on, the founder is the sales team. Outreach is the first thing to slip when the product is on fire: a list in a spreadsheet, a few requests sent on a good day, follow-ups forgotten. Replies end up scattered across tabs.

## A founder's workflow in Spurly

1. **Define your ICP.** One title, one company type, one size.
2. **Build the list.** Search LinkedIn with filters, find people at target companies, or find people who post about your topic. See the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder).
3. **Let AI write and run the sequence.** AI drafts the connection note and follow-ups in your voice, you approve them once, and Spurly sends the request, waits until they accept, messages and follows up. See [campaigns](/product/campaigns).
4. **Reply from one inbox.** When someone answers, the sequence stops for them and you take over in the [inbox](/product/inbox).

## Start with warm leads

Intent signals beat cold lists. Two sources suit a founder with some visibility: people who viewed your profile, and followers of you or your company page. Both can become an audience in a few clicks.

## What you do and what Spurly does

You decide who to reach and approve what goes out. AI drafts the copy, and Spurly handles the timing, the pacing, the follow-ups and the tracking, around the clock with quiet hours if you want them, and it stays within limits designed to protect your account. Read the [safety page](/product/safety) for the honest version of the risk.
