Spurly lets a founder run LinkedIn outreach without an SDR: you pick the people and write the messages, and Spurly sends connection requests and follow-ups at a safe pace and gathers every reply in one inbox. It runs in the cloud, so it keeps working while you build the product.

## The problem

Early on, the founder is the sales team. Outreach is the first thing to slip when the product is on fire: a list in a spreadsheet, a few requests sent on a good day, follow-ups forgotten. Replies end up scattered across tabs.

## A founder's workflow in Spurly

1. **Define one sharp audience.** One title, one company type, one size.
2. **Build the list.** Search LinkedIn with filters, find people at target companies, or find people who post about your topic. See the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder).
3. **Launch a sequence.** Send a connection request, wait until they accept, send a short message, then one follow-up. See [campaigns](/product/campaigns).
4. **Reply from one inbox.** When someone answers, the sequence stops for them and you take over in the [inbox](/product/inbox).

## Reach people who already know you exist

Two sources suit a founder with some visibility: people who viewed your profile, and followers of you or your company page. Both can become an audience in a few clicks.

## What you do and what Spurly does

You decide who to contact and what to say. Spurly handles the timing, the pacing, the follow-ups and the tracking, and it stays within limits designed to protect your account. Read the [safety page](/product/safety) for the honest version of the risk.
