Spurly is an AI SDR for LinkedIn that suits agencies and consultants who want to fill their own pipeline: it finds high-intent leads, writes personalized messages with AI and follows up 24/7 until they reply. It is not a client-management platform, and this page says so up front so you can decide quickly.

## What Spurly does well for an agency

- Finds the people you want to reach from your ICP, with filtered search, companies, posts and CSV imports, plus warm leads such as profile viewers and followers.
- Drafts connection notes and follow-ups with AI for you to approve, then sends them at a paced rate around the clock, even with your laptop closed.
- Stops a sequence as soon as someone replies, and gathers replies in one [inbox](/product/inbox).

## What it does not do

Each user connects one LinkedIn account. There is no shared team workspace and no way to run many clients' accounts from one login. If you run outreach on behalf of clients across several accounts, look at tools designed for agency use, such as [HeyReach](/compare/spurly-vs-heyreach).

## A typical setup

1. Define your ICP for your own services.
2. Build the list in the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder).
3. Launch a [campaign](/product/campaigns) with a short request and one follow-up.
4. Take over in the inbox when someone replies.

## Cost

One plan at $24.99 per month (₹2,499 in India) per user. See [pricing](/pricing).
