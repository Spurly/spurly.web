Spurly puts an AI SDR next to every rep: each rep runs AI-written LinkedIn sequences from their own account and works the replies in one inbox. The AI finds and writes, campaigns run 24/7 in the cloud between meetings, and the rep approves every message and takes the conversations.

## Where reps lose time

Copying names into spreadsheets, remembering who to follow up with, and chasing replies across LinkedIn tabs. The work that wins meetings is the message and the reply. The rest should not need a person.

## A rep's workflow in Spurly

1. **Build a prospect list.** Search with filters, pull people from target accounts, or paste LinkedIn URLs. See the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder).
2. **Run an AI-written sequence.** AI drafts the note and follow-ups in the rep's voice; once approved, Spurly visits the profile, connects, waits until they accept, messages and follows up. See [campaigns](/product/campaigns).
3. **Handle replies.** The sequence stops for anyone who answers, and the reply waits in your [inbox](/product/inbox).
4. **Capture as you browse.** The [Chrome extension](/product/chrome-extension) saves people from LinkedIn and Sales Navigator pages straight into Spurly.

## Intent signals worth acting on

Profile viewers and followers of your page are people who already noticed you. Posts on a topic, and companies that are hiring, point to people with a reason to talk. Each can be turned into an audience.

## Teams and organizations

Each rep connects their own LinkedIn account and runs their own campaigns. If you are rolling Spurly out to a team or an organization, we arrange the plan with you directly. [Book a meeting](/book-demo) and tell us how many people you have and what you need.

## Pricing

The standard plan is per account: $24.99 per month (₹2,499 in India) with a 7-day free trial and everything included. See [pricing](/pricing), or compare [Spurly with Dripify](/compare/spurly-vs-dripify).
