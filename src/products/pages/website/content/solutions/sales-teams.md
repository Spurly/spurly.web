Spurly helps a sales rep run LinkedIn outreach at a steady pace: build a prospect list, send connection requests and follow-ups automatically, and answer replies from one inbox. Each rep works from their own LinkedIn account, and campaigns keep running in the cloud between meetings.

## Where reps lose time

Copying names into spreadsheets, remembering who to follow up with, and chasing replies across LinkedIn tabs. The work that wins meetings is the message and the reply. The rest should not need a person.

## A rep's workflow in Spurly

1. **Build a prospect list.** Search with filters, pull people from target accounts, or paste LinkedIn URLs. See the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder).
2. **Run a sequence.** Visit the profile, connect, wait until they accept, message, follow up. See [campaigns](/product/campaigns).
3. **Handle replies.** The sequence stops for anyone who answers, and the reply waits in your [inbox](/product/inbox).
4. **Capture as you browse.** The [Chrome extension](/product/chrome-extension) saves people from LinkedIn and Sales Navigator pages straight into Spurly.

## Signals worth acting on

Profile viewers and followers of your page are people who already noticed you. Posts on a topic, and companies that are hiring, point to people with a reason to talk. Each can be turned into an audience.

## Teams and organizations

Each rep connects their own LinkedIn account and runs their own campaigns. If you are rolling Spurly out to a team or an organization, we arrange the plan with you directly. [Book a meeting](mailto:founders@getspurly.com?subject=Spurly%20team%20plan) and tell us how many people you have and what you need.
