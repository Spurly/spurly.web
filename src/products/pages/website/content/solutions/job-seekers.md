A job search is an outreach project: you need to reach people at specific companies, and you need to follow up without being annoying. Spurly helps with the repeating parts. The personal parts are still yours.

## A sensible approach

1. **Pick 10 to 20 companies.** Not 200.
2. **Find the right people.** Hiring managers, team leads and people in the role you want. Use the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder) to search by company and title.
3. **Write a short, honest note.** Use the [connection request templates](/templates/linkedin-connection-request-messages), and change the first sentence for each person.
4. **Follow up once.** A [campaign](/product/campaigns) can send a request, wait for acceptance, send a short message and stop on a reply.

## Ask for advice, not a job

People answer questions about their work far more readily than requests for referrals. Ask two specific questions and let the conversation go where it goes.

## Keep it small

Sending to a few dozen well-chosen people is safer for your account and works better. Read [is LinkedIn automation safe?](/blog/is-linkedin-automation-safe) before you start.
