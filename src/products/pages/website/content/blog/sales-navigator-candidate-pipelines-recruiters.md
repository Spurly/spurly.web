Sales Navigator has "sales" in the name, but its real superpower is search — and that makes it one of the best sourcing tools a recruiter can use. With the right filters and a repeatable workflow, you can turn a role into a shortlist of warm candidates in an afternoon.

## Translate the role into filters

Start with the job description and convert it into search filters: current title, seniority, years of experience, industry, location, and even current company (great for poaching from competitors). Sales Navigator's granular filters let you get specific in a way the standard LinkedIn search can't.

## Build a shortlist per role

Treat each open role as its own pipeline. Run the search, then capture the matching profiles into a dedicated list. With [Spurly](/), you can capture the profiles from a Sales Navigator page with the Chrome extension, or build the audience inside Spurly with filtered search. Names, titles, companies and locations land in a tidy list instead of you pasting into a sheet.

## Enrich for follow-up

A name alone isn't enough to run a real outreach sequence. Enriching profiles with contact details gives you a way to follow up beyond the LinkedIn inbox, so strong candidates don't slip through the cracks.

## Reach out like a human, at scale

Candidates get spammed with copy-paste "exciting opportunity" messages all day. Stand out by personalizing: reference their actual experience and why this specific role fits. Let Spurly's AI draft the template from a short brief, edit it so it sounds like you, and let variables tailor each message — the same approach we cover in [personalizing LinkedIn connection requests at scale](/blog/personalize-linkedin-connection-requests). Personal, relevant notes get replies; blasts get ignored.

## Keep it organized

A separate list per role keeps your candidate pipelines clean: who's been contacted, who responded, who's worth a second look. That structure makes you faster on the next role too, because your searches and templates are already built.
