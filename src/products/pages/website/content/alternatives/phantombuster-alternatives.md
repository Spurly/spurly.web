PhantomBuster is a general automation platform. People who only want to run LinkedIn outreach often find it more setup than they need. Facts were checked on 3 October 2026.

## The short list

These are built specifically for LinkedIn outreach:

- **Spurly:** $24.99 a month (₹2,499 in India). See [Spurly vs PhantomBuster](/compare/spurly-vs-phantombuster).
- **Waalaxy:** from €19 a month. See [Spurly vs Waalaxy](/compare/spurly-vs-waalaxy).
- **Dripify:** from $59 a month. See [Spurly vs Dripify](/compare/spurly-vs-dripify).
- **HeyReach:** $79 per sender a month. See [Spurly vs HeyReach](/compare/spurly-vs-heyreach).
- **Lemlist:** from $55 a month, with email. See [Spurly vs Lemlist](/compare/spurly-vs-lemlist).

## What you give up

PhantomBuster is flexible and good at data extraction. The tools above are less flexible but ready to use for outreach on day one.
