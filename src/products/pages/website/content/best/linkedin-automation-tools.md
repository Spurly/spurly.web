There is no single best tool. The right choice depends on how many accounts you run, whether you need email, and how much you want to configure. We make Spurly, so weigh our view accordingly and check each vendor's own site. Prices were re-checked on 17 October 2026.

| Tool | Starting price | Best for | Runs in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spurly | $24.99/month (₹2,499 in India) | An AI SDR for one person: AI-written messages, intent signals, Claude (MCP), LinkedIn only | Cloud |

## Where Spurly fits

Spurly is an AI SDR for LinkedIn: it finds high-intent leads that match your ICP, writes personalized messages with AI and follows up until they reply, while you approve every message. It adds intent signals (profile viewers, followers, post authors, hiring companies), Claude (MCP), invitation autopilot and 24/7 sending with quiet hours to one flat plan. No tool can promise zero risk of a LinkedIn restriction.

## How to pick

1. **How many accounts?** One account: Spurly, Waalaxy, Dripify, Expandi or Linked Helper. Many: HeyReach.
2. **Do you need email?** Lemlist or Waalaxy Business.
3. **Do you want to run it yourself?** Linked Helper. Otherwise a cloud tool.
4. **Do you need branching?** Expandi.
