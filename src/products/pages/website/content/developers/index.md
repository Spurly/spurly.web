The Spurly LinkedIn API lets your product work with your customers' LinkedIn accounts: connect an account, read and send messages, search, view profiles, send invitations and publish posts. Spurly runs the sign-in, verification and session health, and calls LinkedIn on your behalf.

Each of your customers is an **organisation**. Everything is one REST API with signed webhooks.
