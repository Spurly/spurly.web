**Social Selling Index (SSI)** is a score from 0 to 100 that LinkedIn gives your account to show how effectively you use the platform for selling. It is made of four parts, each scored up to 25.

## The four components

1. **Establish your professional brand:** a complete profile and useful content.
2. **Find the right people:** how well you search for and identify prospects.
3. **Engage with insights:** sharing and interacting with relevant content.
4. **Build relationships:** growing your network with decision-makers.

## Does it matter?

LinkedIn presents SSI as an indicator of social selling effort. It is not a ranking factor you can rely on for reach, and it does not tell you whether a message will be answered. Treat it as a rough habit tracker, not a goal.

## What actually helps outreach

A clear profile, a targeted list and short relevant messages affect replies far more than the score. See the [LinkedIn outreach guide](/blog/linkedin-outreach-guide).
