**Intent signals** are actions or facts that suggest someone may be ready to talk: they looked at you, follow you, wrote about the problem you solve, or work at a company hiring for a related role. They help you start with [warm leads](/glossary/warm-outreach) instead of a cold list.

## Intent signals Spurly surfaces on LinkedIn

- **Viewed your profile:** people who already checked you out.
- **Follow you or your company page:** the audience that already knows who you are.
- **Posting about your topic:** people talking about the problem you solve; their authors can be imported.
- **Hiring for a related role:** companies with an open role have a need.
- **Invited you:** people who send you an invitation, which Spurly can auto-accept when their headline matches your keywords.

Each one becomes a list you can add to a campaign from the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder). Spurly does not score or rank people automatically.

## General B2B signals

Beyond LinkedIn, sales teams also watch signals such as recent funding, leadership changes, new tools adopted or visits to a pricing page. These are general examples, and Spurly does not collect them.

## Why they matter

People respond better when the message arrives for a reason. A signal gives you that reason and a better first sentence, which usually helps [connection acceptance rate](/glossary/connection-acceptance-rate) too. An [AI SDR](/glossary/ai-sdr) uses signals to decide who to reach first.
