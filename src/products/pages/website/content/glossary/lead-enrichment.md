**Lead enrichment** is the process of adding information to a lead you already have so you can target and personalise your outreach. A lead that starts as a name and a profile link becomes a person with a title, a company and other details.

## Why it matters

Messages that mention a person's role, company or recent activity get more replies than generic ones. Enrichment makes that possible at scale, without opening every profile by hand.

## What gets added

Typically a headline, current company and role, location and other public profile details. What is available depends on the source and on what the person has made public.

## In Spurly

Each lead moves through queued, enriching, enriched or failed, so you can see which ones are ready to use in a message. A failed lead can be retried. Read more on the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder) page and in the [outreach guide](/blog/linkedin-outreach-guide).
