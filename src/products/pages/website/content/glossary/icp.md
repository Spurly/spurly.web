**ICP** stands for ideal customer profile: a description of the kind of company, and the kind of person within it, that gets the most value from your product and is most likely to buy.

## What goes in one

- **Company:** industry, size, location, stage
- **Person:** job title, seniority, function
- **Signals:** hiring for a role, recent funding, topics they post about
- **Disqualifiers:** who you do not want to reach

## ICP versus persona

An ICP describes the company type. A persona describes an individual buyer inside it. Outreach needs both, since you search by company traits and write to a person.

## ICP in LinkedIn outreach

A tight ICP keeps your list small and relevant, which raises [acceptance rate](/glossary/connection-acceptance-rate) and protects your account. Turn your ICP into filters in the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder). Start with one sharp audience, not three loose ones.
