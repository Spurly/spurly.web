**InMail** is a LinkedIn message you can send to a person you are not connected to. It is a paid feature: credits come with Premium plans such as Sales Navigator and Recruiter, and the number you get depends on the plan.

## How it differs from a normal message

A normal message needs a connection. InMail skips that step, so it suits recruiters and sales reps who want to reach someone before connecting. Some members also have open profiles that anyone can message for free.

## When it makes sense

InMail works when the person is hard to reach any other way and you have a clear reason to write. For most outreach, a [connection request](/glossary/connection-acceptance-rate) with a short note followed by a message is cheaper and gets a similar or better response.

## InMail and automation

Spurly does not send InMail. It sends connection requests and messages to people you are connected to. See the [LinkedIn outreach guide](/blog/linkedin-outreach-guide) for how the standard flow works.
