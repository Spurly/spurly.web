**Connection acceptance rate** is the percentage of LinkedIn connection requests you send that the recipient accepts.

## How it is calculated

Accepted requests divided by sent requests, over a fixed period. If you send 100 requests and 30 are accepted, your acceptance rate is 30%. Count only requests old enough to have had a chance to be answered, since many people take days or weeks to respond.

## What counts as a good rate

There is no official benchmark. Rates vary a lot by audience, by whether you add a note and by how relevant you are to the person. Compare your rate with your own earlier campaigns, not with a number from a blog post.

## What moves it

- **Relevance:** a shared topic, group or connection helps most.
- **Profile quality:** people check who you are before accepting.
- **The note:** short and specific usually beats long and generic. Sometimes no note at all does better, so test.
- **Targeting:** a precise list beats a big one.

A low rate is also a safety signal. If many people ignore or flag requests, LinkedIn may take notice, which is why tight targeting matters.
