An **AI SDR** (AI sales development rep) does the top-of-funnel work a human SDR does: it finds people who match your ideal customer profile, writes personalized outreach and follows up until they reply. Spurly is an AI SDR for LinkedIn: it finds high-intent leads that match your ICP, writes personalized messages with AI and follows up until they reply, while you approve every message.

## What an AI SDR does

- **Finds leads** that fit your [ICP](/glossary/icp), and ideally the warm ones, using [intent signals](/glossary/intent-signals).
- **Writes outreach** such as a connection note and follow-ups, personalized for each person.
- **Follows up** on a schedule and stops when the person replies.
- **Hands over** the conversation to a human.

## AI SDR vs AI-assisted tools vs a human SDR

| | Human SDR | AI-assisted tool | AI SDR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finds leads | Yes, by hand | You build the list | From your ICP and signals |
| Writes messages | Yes | Suggests text | Drafts them for approval |
| Follows up | Yes, as time allows | You trigger it | On schedule, around the clock |
| Takes the conversation | Yes | You | You |

## AI SDR on LinkedIn

On LinkedIn the work is visiting profiles, sending connection requests, messaging after an accept and following up. Spurly runs these as [campaigns and sequences](/product/campaigns) from the cloud, 24/7, with quiet hours if you want them. Replies land in one inbox, and you can connect Claude through [MCP](/product/ai-assistants) to sort replies and draft answers, which you approve before they are sent. Spurly does not reply to leads on its own.

## What to look for

- **Signals:** does it start from warm leads or only a cold list?
- **Personalization:** can you see and edit every draft?
- **Approval:** does anything go out without your say-so?
- **Safety:** are there limits per action, uneven gaps and a stop on reply? No tool can promise zero risk; see the [safety page](/product/safety).
