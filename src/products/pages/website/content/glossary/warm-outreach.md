**Warm outreach** means contacting someone who already has some reason to recognise you, such as a profile view, a like on your post, a shared group or a mutual connection. **Cold outreach** has none of these.

## Why warm usually works better

A warm contact has a reason to say yes. Acceptance and reply rates are typically higher, and you can open with the shared context instead of an introduction.

## Warm sources on LinkedIn

- People who viewed your profile
- Followers of you or your company page
- People who engaged with a post on your topic
- People in the same group or event
- Second-degree connections through someone you know

## How to write to a warm contact

Mention the shared context in the first sentence and keep the ask small. For cold messages, see [LinkedIn cold message structure](/blog/linkedin-cold-message-structure).
