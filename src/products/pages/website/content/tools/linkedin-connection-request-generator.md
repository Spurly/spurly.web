Fill in a few details about the person you want to reach and get a short, personal connection request you can copy straight into LinkedIn. Every note stays under LinkedIn's 300-character limit.

## How to get a good note

The tool works best when you give it something specific to point at. A shared interest, a post they wrote or a reason you are contacting them beats a generic "I'd like to add you to my network". Keep the "what you do" line to one short sentence.

## What makes a connection request work

A good request is short, names a real reason, asks for nothing big and is easy to say yes to. The longer version, with examples, is in [LinkedIn connection request examples](/blog/linkedin-connection-request-examples), and the ready-to-edit versions are in the [connection request templates](/templates/linkedin-connection-request-messages).
