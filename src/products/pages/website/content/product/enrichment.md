A lead from a search often has little more than a name and a headline. Enrichment fills in the rest, such as title, company and work history, so your message can mention something true about the person.

## Pick the leads that matter

Open the "Needs enrichment" tab on your leads page, select the people you want and enrich them from the selection. You only enrich people you plan to contact.

## It runs in the background

Each batch is queued and processed in the background. Leave the page and come back whenever you like. Every lead shows a clear status: queued, enriching, enriched or failed. Failed leads can be retried.

## Use what you learn

Enriched details become variables in your messages, with a live preview before anything sends. A message that says "I saw you lead sales at [Company]" is much more likely to get a reply than "Hi there". See [campaigns](/product/campaigns) and the glossary entry on [lead enrichment](/glossary/lead-enrichment).

## Where leads come from

Search, companies, posts, jobs, pasted URLs, profile viewers, followers and CSV imports. See the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder).
