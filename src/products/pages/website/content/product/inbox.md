Spurly's inbox puts every LinkedIn conversation in one place, so replies to your campaigns never get lost between tabs. You read, reply, react and send files from the same screen where you run your outreach.


![The Spurly inbox with conversations, a thread and a reply box. Names and messages are blurred.](/assets/app-inbox.webp)

## All your conversations in one list

Your LinkedIn conversations appear in a two-pane inbox: the list of conversations on one side and the thread on the other. Replies to messages sent by your [campaigns and sequences](/product/campaigns) land here alongside every other conversation, so there is one place to check instead of several.

## Reply, react and send files

Reply to any conversation, react to a message, and send or receive files and PDFs without leaving Spurly. You answer from the inbox as you would on LinkedIn, with the context of the campaign that started the conversation close by.

## New messages as they arrive

Spurly receives new messages on its servers as they arrive, so you do not have to refresh or keep a LinkedIn tab open to see them.

## Replies stop your sequences

When someone answers a sequence message, Spurly stops the sequence for that person. You never have an automated follow-up land in the middle of a real conversation. The reply is in your inbox, ready for you.

## What the inbox does not do

LinkedIn does not provide read receipts, so Spurly cannot show whether someone has read your message. The inbox shows your standard LinkedIn conversations.

## How to start

1. **Connect your LinkedIn account** once, through a secure connection.
2. **Open the inbox.** Your conversations are there.
3. **Reply from Spurly.** Send text, reactions or files.
