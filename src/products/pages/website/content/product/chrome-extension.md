The Spurly Chrome extension saves profiles from LinkedIn and Sales Navigator pages into Spurly in one click. It is now one way to fill your audience, not the whole product: once people are in Spurly, [campaigns](/product/campaigns) send the outreach from the cloud.

## Capture while you browse

Open a LinkedIn or Sales Navigator page, including search results, tick the people you want and push them to Spurly. Names, titles, companies and locations land in your contacts instead of a spreadsheet you paste into.

## Three tabs in one panel

- **Capture:** pick the profiles you want from the page you are on.
- **Outreach:** send connection requests through a real Spurly campaign, so the activity is tracked and paced like everything else.
- **Templates:** write and keep message templates with variables, with a live preview.

## Works with the rest of Spurly

Captured people appear in your contacts and can join any campaign or sequence. The extension signs in with your Spurly account, so there is nothing separate to set up. Spurly skips anyone you have already invited, whichever way the invitation was sent.

## How to start

1. **Install the extension** from the Chrome Web Store.
2. **Sign in** with your Spurly account.
3. **Open LinkedIn** and capture the people you want.
4. **Launch a campaign** with them from Spurly.

Prefer to skip the extension? The [lead finder](/product/lead-finder) builds audiences inside Spurly with filtered search, company search, posts, pasted URLs and CSV import.
