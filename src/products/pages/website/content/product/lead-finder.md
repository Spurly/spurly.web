Intent signals on LinkedIn are the actions that show someone may be ready to talk. Spurly's lead finder, a LinkedIn lead generation tool, builds an audience from your ICP and surfaces five of them: people who viewed your profile, follow you or your company page, posted about your topic, work at a company that is hiring, or invited you. Spurly lists these people for you to review and does not score or rank them.

![The Spurly leads table with a bulk-action bar and degree, status and enrichment columns.](/assets/app-leads.webp)

## Define your ICP: filtered LinkedIn people search

Search LinkedIn people by the filters you would use on LinkedIn itself, then import the results into Spurly. This uses standard LinkedIn search, so you do not need Sales Navigator.

## Discover: start from companies, posts or jobs

- **Companies:** search for companies, then pull in the people who work there.
- **Posts:** search for posts on a topic and import their authors. People who write about your topic are people who care about it.
- **Jobs:** use job searches as a hiring signal, since a company that is hiring is a company with a need.

## Paste any LinkedIn URL

Have one profile in mind? Paste its LinkedIn URL and Spurly imports it as a lead.

## The intent signals Spurly surfaces

A cold list gets ignored. These start from people who already showed interest in you or in the problem you solve.

- **Profile viewers** become leads. Free LinkedIn accounts see a limited number of viewers, which is LinkedIn's limit, not Spurly's.
- **Followers** of you or of your company page can be an audience.
- **Post authors** are people who write about your topic (see Discover above).
- **Hiring companies** have an open role, which points to a real need.
- **People who invited you** appear in your [invitations](/product/network), where you can auto-accept the ones whose headline matches your ICP keywords.
- **Your network** is synced, so every first-degree connection is in Spurly as well.

## Bring your own list

Import a CSV and map its columns to Spurly fields, or use the [Chrome extension](/product/chrome-extension) to capture profiles while you browse LinkedIn and Sales Navigator pages.

## How to build your first audience

1. **Connect your LinkedIn account.**
2. **Choose a source:** a filtered search, a company, a topic, a URL or a CSV.
3. **Review the people** Spurly found and keep the ones you want.
4. **Start a [campaign](/product/campaigns)** with them.
