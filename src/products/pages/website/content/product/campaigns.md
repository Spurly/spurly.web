Spurly campaigns send LinkedIn connection requests and follow-ups for you, from the cloud, at a safe daily pace. You choose the people and write the messages. Spurly handles the timing, notices when someone accepts, and stops when someone replies, even with your laptop closed.


![A running Spurly campaign: delivery status, hourly and weekly limits, sending window and the queue. Names are blurred.](/assets/app-campaign-detail.webp)

## What a campaign does

Pick an audience, write your connection note and start the campaign. Spurly sends one request at a time, spread across your working hours in your own time zone, instead of firing a burst that looks nothing like a person.

Each person is contacted once. Spurly skips anyone you have already invited, whether that invitation came from Spurly or from the [Chrome extension](/product/chrome-extension). If LinkedIn disconnects your account, the campaign pauses rather than working through your list with a dead connection.

## Multi-step sequences

A sequence is a short, ordered list of steps that runs for each person. You can combine these steps:

- Visit their profile
- Send a connection request
- Send a message
- Follow them
- Like one of their posts
- Comment on one of their posts
- Endorse a skill (only possible for people you are already connected to; Spurly skips the step for everyone else)
- Wait, for a fixed number of days or until they accept

A typical sequence: send a connection request, wait until they accept, send a short message, wait three days, then send one follow-up. Steps always run in the order you set. There is no branching canvas to maintain.

## It stops when someone replies

Before each step, Spurly checks the conversation. If the person's message is the latest one in the thread, the sequence stops for that person instead of sending the next scripted message. Replies go to your [unified inbox](/product/inbox), where you answer them yourself.

## Acceptance is detected automatically

Spurly checks your connections automatically and marks people as connected when they accept. That is what lets a "wait until they accept" step move on without you checking. You also get a notification when a connection request is accepted.

## How to launch your first campaign

1. **Connect your LinkedIn account.** You sign in once through a secure connection.
2. **Build an audience.** Search LinkedIn, find people at target companies, paste a LinkedIn URL or import a CSV. See the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder).
3. **Write your message.** Use a template with variables such as first name, with a live preview before anything sends.
4. **Start the campaign.** Follow progress on the campaign page. Pause it whenever you like.

## Built to stay within LinkedIn's limits

Every automated action passes through caps and pacing rules before it is sent, and the details are on the [safety page](/product/safety). The honest version: LinkedIn's User Agreement restricts automation, and no tool can promise an account will never be restricted. Spurly is designed to keep your activity well inside the limits LinkedIn applies to normal use.
