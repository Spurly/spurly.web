Spurly is AI LinkedIn outreach that follows up for you: AI writes your connection note and follow-ups in your voice, you approve them, and Spurly sends them 24/7 and stops when someone replies. It runs from the cloud, so campaigns keep going with your laptop closed.

![A running Spurly campaign: delivery status, hourly and weekly limits, sending window and the queue. Names are blurred.](/assets/app-campaign-detail.webp)

## What a campaign does

Pick an audience, let AI draft your connection note and follow-ups, approve them and start the campaign. Spurly sends one request at a time, spread out around the clock with uneven gaps, instead of firing a burst that looks nothing like a person.

Each person is contacted once. Spurly skips anyone you contacted in the last 30 days across your campaigns, and anyone you have already invited, whether that invitation came from Spurly or from the [Chrome extension](/product/chrome-extension). If LinkedIn disconnects your account, the campaign pauses rather than working through your list with a dead connection.

## AI writes the messages, you approve them

Describe who you are writing to and Spurly's AI drafts a personalized connection note and the follow-ups in your voice. Placeholders such as first name and company fill in for each person when the message is sent. You review and edit every draft before the campaign starts, so nothing goes out that you have not approved. AI can also draft comments on your leads' posts for you to approve.

## Multi-step sequences

A sequence is a short, ordered list of steps that runs for each person. You can combine these steps:

- Visit their profile
- Send a connection request
- Send a message
- Follow them
- Like one of their posts
- Comment on one of their posts
- Endorse a skill (only possible for people you are already connected to; Spurly skips the step for everyone else)
- Wait, for a fixed number of days or until they accept

A typical sequence: send a connection request, wait until they accept, send a short message, wait three days, then send one follow-up. Steps always run in the order you set. There is no branching canvas to maintain.

## Runs 24/7, on your hours

Campaigns and sequences run around the clock, weekends included, so you do not have to be online for them to work, and replies reach your inbox the moment they arrive. Prefer nights off? Switch on quiet hours, pick your window in your own timezone, and Spurly sends roughly four times further apart while it is quiet instead of stopping.

## It stops when someone replies

Before each step, Spurly checks the conversation. If the person's message is the latest one in the thread, the sequence stops for that person instead of sending the next scripted message. Replies go to your [unified inbox](/product/inbox), where you answer them yourself.

## Acceptance is detected automatically

Spurly checks your connections automatically and marks people as connected when they accept. That is what lets a "wait until they accept" step move on without you checking. You also get a notification when a connection request is accepted.

## How to launch your first campaign

1. **Connect your LinkedIn account.** You sign in once through a secure connection.
2. **Build an audience.** Search LinkedIn, find people at target companies, paste a LinkedIn URL or import a CSV. See the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder).
3. **Write your message.** Let AI draft the note and follow-ups in your voice, or use your own template with variables such as first name. Either way you get a live preview and approve it before anything sends.
4. **Start the campaign.** Follow progress on the campaign page. Pause it whenever you like.

## Built to stay within LinkedIn's limits

Connection requests pass through daily and hourly caps and pacing rules before they are sent, and the details are on the [safety page](/product/safety). The honest version: LinkedIn's User Agreement restricts automation, and no tool can promise an account will never be restricted. Spurly is designed to keep your activity well inside the limits LinkedIn applies to normal use.
