Spurly's LinkedIn MCP server lets Claude run your outreach in plain language, so Spurly works as an AI SDR you talk to. Claude can read your results, draft campaigns and, only if you turn it on, act, and you ask for things like "how did my outreach do this week?", "which replies need an answer?" or "draft a campaign for heads of growth at fintechs in London".

## What an assistant can do

- **Read.** See your campaigns, sequences, leads, inbox and numbers. It cannot change anything.
- **Draft.** Create campaigns, sequences and message templates as drafts. Nothing is sent or started.
- **Act.** Start a campaign, send a reply or answer an invitation. This needs two switches: the Act permission when you connect, and "Allow AI assistants to take actions" in your settings, which is off until you turn it on.

You pick the permissions on a consent screen when you connect, and you can grant less than the app asks for.

## Reply triage with Claude

Claude can sort the replies in your [inbox](/product/inbox), tell you which need an answer today and draft responses. You approve each draft, and nothing goes out on its own.

## Ready-made starting points

Spurly adds a few prompts to your assistant: a weekly outreach review, a reply triage, and a campaign planner. Your saved message templates are also readable, so the assistant can reuse your wording.

## You stay in control

- Sending counts against the same daily and weekly limits as the rest of Spurly, so an assistant cannot send in a burst.
- Every connection can be disconnected from Settings, and personal tokens can be revoked in one click.
- Recent assistant activity is listed in Settings so you can see what was done and when.

An assistant reads text from LinkedIn, such as bios and messages, and that text can be written to mislead it. Keep the Act permission for assistants you trust, and ask yours to show you a draft before it sends. The [safety page](/product/safety) explains how pacing and limits work.

## Get started

Add the Spurly server to your assistant and approve the connection. The [setup guide](/product/ai-assistants-setup) covers Claude, Claude Code and Cursor. Spurly has a 7-day free trial, and assistants work during the trial.
