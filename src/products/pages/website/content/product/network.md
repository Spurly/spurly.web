Spurly can auto-accept incoming LinkedIn invitations, from everyone or only when the inviter's headline matches keywords you set, and auto-withdraw invitations nobody accepted after the number of days you choose. Both rules are off until you turn them on, and Spurly also keeps a current list of everyone you are connected to.

## Your connections in one place

Sync once and Spurly pulls in your connections, then keeps the list current in the background. Search by name. Title and company appear once a connection has been [enriched](/product/enrichment). The page shows when it last checked and when it will next.

## Invitations you sent

The Invitations page lists requests still waiting, how long each has been pending and the note you sent. Requests pending for three weeks or more are flagged as stale. You can withdraw them one by one.

## Withdraw stale requests automatically

An old pending request does you no good, and a large pile of them is a poor look. Turn on automatic withdrawal and Spurly withdraws requests that have been pending longer than the number of days you set (7 to 90, default 21). It is off until you switch it on, and each withdrawal counts toward the daily limits described on the [safety page](/product/safety).

## Invitations you received

Accept or decline requests sent to you. Answers are sent from your account and counted toward a daily limit to keep it safe.

## Auto-accept the right invitations

Turn on auto-accept and choose a rule: accept everyone, or accept only when the inviter's headline contains one of up to 20 keywords, such as "founder" or "head of growth". Invitations that do not match stay in your list for you to decide. People who invite you are warm leads, so this keeps your network growing without you checking every request.

## Audiences from your network

Your synced connections, profile viewers and followers of you or your company page can each become an audience for a [campaign](/product/campaigns). See the [lead finder](/product/lead-finder).

## How it fits with campaigns

Acceptance is detected automatically, so a [campaign](/product/campaigns) step that waits for someone to accept can move on without you checking.
