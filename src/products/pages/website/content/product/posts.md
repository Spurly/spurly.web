To schedule LinkedIn posts, write the post in Spurly and pick a date: text posts can be scheduled up to 60 days ahead, and a post with an image or video is published right away. Scheduled posts warm up the people your outreach reaches, since prospects look at your profile before they reply, and they go out from your own LinkedIn account.

## Schedule text posts

Write the post, pick a date and time, and Spurly publishes it for you. Text posts can be scheduled up to 60 days ahead.

## Publish an image or a video

You can attach one image or one video. Posts with media are published right away and cannot be scheduled.

## A sensible posting pace

Posts go out only when you schedule them or publish them yourself, so the pace is yours to set. How Spurly paces other actions is on the [safety page](/product/safety).

## Why posts help outreach

People check your profile before accepting a connection request or answering a message. Recent, relevant posts make that check easier to pass. Pair them with [campaigns](/product/campaigns) for the outreach itself.
